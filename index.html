Selected work

Four projects spanning high-voltage power, embedded control, industrial safety logic, and signal processing.

Active Mar 2026 — Present

96V Contactor Driver & Precharge Board

UCI Solar Car · Motor Engineer

Designed and fabricated a custom PCB with MOSFET gate-driver circuits switching the DC-DC, precharge, and 96V main contactors, isolating low-voltage control signals from the vehicle's high-voltage bus. Sized the precharge power resistors and contactor timing to limit inrush current into the motor controller's capacitor bank, then validated switching behavior on an oscilloscope before vehicle integration.

  • Altium
  • MOSFET gate drivers
  • High-voltage
  • Oscilloscope bring-up

Completed Nov 2025 — May 2026

ESP32-S3 Multi-Motor Control Board

FUSION Engineering Project · Controls Engineer

Built a custom ESP32-S3 control PCB integrating five H-bridge motor drivers, per-channel current sensing, and 5V/3.3V regulation to run four DC motors, two servos, and a pump on a fire-extinguishing rover. Wrote the embedded firmware in C++ with Bluepad32, translating wireless gamepad input into coordinated real-time speed and direction control.

  • ESP32-S3
  • KiCad
  • H-bridge drivers
  • C++ / Bluepad32
Active Mar 2026 — Present

Redundant Ride Safety Architecture

Toronto Metropolitan Thrill Design Competition · Controls Engineer

Specified a redundant safety architecture integrating Hall-effect, inductive proximity, load, pressure, and vehicle-tracking sensors into a central PLC for fault detection and automated Emergency Stop. Developed the ladder logic for the full ride control system, including redundant sensor validation, dispatch and E-Stop interlocks, and synchronized audio, lighting, and show effects.

  • PLC ladder logic
  • Sensor redundancy
  • Functional safety
Completed Sept 2025 — Dec 2025

Invisible Fence — Spatial Audio Filtering

Digital Signal Processing Project

Designed an LTI digital filtering system in MATLAB to isolate a desired audio source and suppress interfering frequency content from nearby sources. Used FFT-based spectral analysis and convolution to identify dominant noise frequencies, characterize the filter's frequency response, and evaluate suppression in both the time and frequency domains.

  • MATLAB
  • FFT
  • LTI systems
  • Filter design
